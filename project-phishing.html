Phishing Email Detection and Awareness

Project 01 · Social engineering awareness

Project overview

I carried out a project on phishing email detection to understand how hackers use fake emails to steal passwords and personal information.

What I did

  • Collected sample phishing emails from free online resources.
  • Checked for fake sender addresses and urgent language.
  • Looked for suspicious links, poor grammar, and unexpected attachments.
  • Practised verifying sender addresses, previewing links without clicking, and reporting phishing emails in Gmail.
  • Completed the Google Phishing Quiz.

Result and learning

I scored 9/10 in the Google Phishing Quiz. I also created a simple checklist to help people identify suspicious emails. This project increased my interest in cybersecurity, especially social engineering attacks.
