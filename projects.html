PROJECT 01

Phishing Email Detection and Awareness

I analysed sample phishing emails to identify warning signs such as fake sender addresses, urgent language, suspicious links, poor grammar, and unexpected attachments. I also practised checking sender addresses, previewing links safely, and reporting phishing emails in Gmail.

PROJECT 02

Password Security Audit

I reviewed password strength by considering length, uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. I also learned about using unique passwords, password managers, and enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).

PROJECT 03

Social Media Privacy Audit

I reviewed privacy settings, audience visibility, location sharing, and third-party app access on my social media accounts. I adjusted settings to limit public access to personal information and removed apps I did not recognise.
